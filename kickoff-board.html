Fashion recs: minimum spec, happy path to the wow

1 · Your Instagram

Type a handle, or drop in 9 screenshots.

Demo: one teammate's own account, preloaded.

2 · Your taste in 3 sentences ★ WOW: "that's me"

"Black that isn't loud. Structure with one soft edge. Silver, never gold."

Each line shows the posts it came from.

Build: vision model → trait tags → 3 sentences.

3 · Fix it (optional)

Tap "yes" or "not me" on each trait. One tap rewrites the sentences.

4 · Pick the occasion

Gala · Info session · Alumni event, plus the date.

Adds the dress code and Chicago weather (coat in November).

5 · Three looks ★ second wow

From a hand-picked catalog of ~30 items.

Each card: why it's you · why it fits the room. One bolder pick.

Not in the demo: login · live Instagram API (not available to us) · real shopping or checkout · other people's accounts · more than 3 occasions

Wow test before building: run 5 classmates' screenshots through step 2. If 3 of 5 say "that's me," the idea holds.

Metric for judges: % who say "that's me" · % who save a look.
